Klairilia VISION Policies

1. Wig Install Preparation

For wig installs, hair must already be braided and washed before your appointment.

2. Quick Weave Preparation Fee

If your hair is not pre-braided for a quick weave, an additional $15 fee will be added.

3. Deposit Requirement

A $50 non-refundable deposit must be paid before your appointment is confirmed.

No Call / No Show Policy

Clients who do not call or show up for their appointment will be banned from booking future appointments.

Montreal Transportation Fee

Clients located in Montreal will have an additional $100 transportation fee.

Ottawa Transportation Fee

Clients located in Ottawa will have an additional $75 transportation fee.

Previous Hairstyles

Hair must not have a previous hairstyle still installed or intact before your appointment.

Mobile Hairstylist Only

Klairilia VISION is a mobile hairstyling service.

Cancellation Policy

Appointments must be cancelled at least 48 hours in advance.

Hair Must Be Washed

Hair must already be washed before your appointment.
